ABOUT PRINTUZ

Making e-commerce fulfillment simple & scalable.

Founded in 2022, Printuz LTD is a fulfillment and e-commerce solutions company based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

We connect online sellers, brands, and e-commerce businesses with trusted production and fulfillment partners across Vietnam, China, and the United States.

Our mission is to make it easier for businesses to create, customize, fulfill, and deliver products to customers around the world — without the complexity of traditional inventory management.
