General

About Printuz

Printuz LTD is a fulfillment and e-commerce solutions company founded in 2022 and based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

We connect e-commerce sellers with production and fulfillment partners across Vietnam, China, and the United States.

Printuz provides solutions for Print-on-Demand (POD), dropshipping, product sourcing, and order fulfillment.

Our goal is to help sellers simplify their supply chain and scale their e-commerce businesses.

Printuz LTD is based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

Products

Print-on-Demand

Print-on-Demand allows products to be produced when an order is placed instead of requiring sellers to purchase and store large quantities of inventory in advance.

This can help sellers launch new products and designs while reducing inventory risk.

Our network supports a broad range of product categories, depending on availability and production location.

ApparelBagsAccessoriesFootwear Home & LivingDrinkwareJewelrySports Products Personalized GiftsSeasonal Products

Many products can support customization, including custom designs and personalized products. Availability depends on the specific product and production facility.

Please contact our team if you have a specific customization requirement.
